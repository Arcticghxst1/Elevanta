# Elevanta
Elevanta is a website that will use AI to help the needs of Business Analysts and Stakeholders to help increase the outcome of a business. For business analysts it will give them alerts to let them know if there are any issues within the business such as decreasing profits or if productivity within different levels and sectors of a business are decreasing and giving the analyst strategies and ideas on how to fix these issues. It will also provide them with AI generated graphs that they would require on different needs. (This is currently the baseline thought process behind the website it will go into more depth as the idea grows). For stakeholders, it will allow them to also see the statistics, strengths and weaknesses of their business and create ideas on how to improve. It would also help stakeholders create blueprint plans that can be put into place based on the goals of the business and calculating the possible success rate of the project allowing it to be sent down to the business analysts after to implement the plans into place.
